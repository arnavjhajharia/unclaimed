**Logline (1 line):** One rainy night as the clerk of a tiny railway station's lost property office. Every object
remembers something, and what you do with each one decides whether you ever leave.

**Description (short):**
Unclaimed is a ten-minute interactive story with three endings. You work the night window at Kestrel Halt. A single red
mitten, an umbrella left behind on purpose, a cassette made for a brother in hospital and a wedding ring in a ticket
machine all turn up during the night. Each one shows you a memory, and you choose what to do with it: give it back, tell
the truth, or keep it. A hidden ledger tracks your warmth, honesty and how much you hold on to. At 05:52 the first train
opens its doors, and which ways out are open to you depends on how you spent the night. The game plays in the browser
with keyboard or touch, and its rain-and-piano ambience is synthesised live in WebAudio.

**AI tools / disclosure (paste in the tools/notes field):**
- Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5 model): all 14 scene illustrations, from original prompts with one shared style line for
  consistency. About 4 credits in total.
- ElevenLabs (via Higgsfield text-to-speech, ElevenLabs engine): all 9 voiced passages (6 object "memories" + 3 ending lines,
  ~1.6 min of speech, voice "Helena"). Tool tag: ElevenLabs. Proof: the Higgsfield generation history shows variant=elevenlabs.
- Claude (Anthropic): co-wrote the code and helped draft the story.
- No third-party IP, no real people, and no samples. The music and ambience are procedural code.
